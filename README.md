# Crack Spread Flow Desk

Educational simulator of a flow trading desk handling a refiner's request to hedge its refining margin.

The desk goes long a 3:2:1 crack spread (short crude, long gasoline and diesel futures) for 21 trading days, and the app shows:

- simulated, correlated futures prices for crude, gasoline and diesel
- the daily P&L, split between price moves and roll yield
- basis and curve risk
- the effect of volatility and correlation scenarios

All prices are simulated. This is a learning project, not trading advice.
